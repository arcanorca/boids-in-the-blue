Hand-cleaned text art of the "terminal" theme (everything before the first header is a comment). All art faces RIGHT;
the generator mirrors the CHARACTER GRID (glyphs swapped) for the left-facing art. A section is
"=== name ===", text rows, a line holding only "--", then tone rows (b bright, n normal, m mid, d dim; a space or a
missing row means normal). "F" / "f" in a fish are the variant's body characters, "L" in the angler marks the lure cell.
The drafts came from tools/terminal-img2ascii.py (shape-matched glyphs of the Refined Pixel sprites) and were cleaned
by hand: eye, fins, mouth, belly line, stray characters. Every glyph must survive a mirror (SAFE in the generator).
