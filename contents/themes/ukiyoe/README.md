# Ukiyo-e (Japanese woodblock print)

The sea as a woodblock print: Bero-ai indigo ink `#0f1926` on washi paper `#e4dbc4`. The default set is exactly
those two colours plus transparency; `img/` holds it. An optional Nishiki-e set (`img-nishikie/`) draws the same
creatures in traditional pigments. `theme.js` `PALETTE_SET = "ink"` selects the default; `"nishikie"` selects the
colour set. Textures are generated by `tools/gen-ukiyoe-theme.js` (`NISHIKIE=1` for the pigment set).

`tests/ukiyoe-theme.test.js` checks the textures, the water rules, and the facing rule; `tests/ukiyoe-measure.js`
measures the comfort criteria on offscreen renders.

## Scale

- One texel is a whole number of device pixels: the screen is `GRID_ROWS = 360` texels tall (3 px per texel at 1080p).
- Backdrop textures are drawn at that scale in a texel world (`Ambient.qml`); creatures are placed in screen
  pixels so they glide, but are drawn at the same texel size.

## Water: Ryuusen strokes and sunbeams

- Four Bokashi depth bands (texel rows 30-42, 49-105, 113-194, 204-282) of undulating one-texel strokes, pitched
  6, 7, 9 and 13 texels apart. Each stroke follows the Ryuusen offset of its column (a sinusoidal wave with a
  160-texel period, amplitude multiplied by `WATER.waveAmp`), and a vertical texel joins the steps, so each stroke
  is one line. No stroke is shorter than 7 texels; gaps are at least 7.
- Five Komorebi sunbeams are diagonal bands where the strokes are denser (`beamBoost`). The two lower bands only
  paint inside the sunbeams.
- Six nested daylight layers (`water-0` night .. `water-5` day). A stroke has an intensity; it appears in layer n
  when the intensity is at most `0.4 + 0.6 n / 5`, so strokes appear once and never move. Night keeps bands 0
  and 2 (flagged `night: true` in theme.js); bands 1 and 3 appear with daylight.
- Paper share of the water: 0.7 % of the screen at night, 3.4 % in full day.
- The top band starts at row 30, below the surface strip. An earlier start at row 12 put a period-2 pattern
  under the strip, which the comfort test flagged.



## Surface

`surface.png` is a 128x10 Hokusai-style strip: wave crests with curling foam claws every 32 texels and an
undertow contour five rows below. It starts at row 17 (51 px); its topmost crest is on row 18 (54 px), so it sits
just below the 54 px top panel. The simulation keeps creatures below texel 31 (`SURFACE.floor`).

## Seabed and kelp

- `seabed.png` is a 640x32 layered strip: matsushima-style rocks, sango coral branches, and hitode sea stars. It
  stays at 8.9 % of the screen height.
- Nine wakame kelp plants (`kelp-<class>-<frame>.png`, three height classes, 17.8 % of the height at most) with
  carved midrib veins and ink outlines, standing on the seabed ridge (`seabedTop`).

## Creatures

- Fish have five size classes and five patterns, named for Japanese prints: stripe = Shimadai bands, spot =
  Hoshidai rosettes, solid = Madai gill arch, hollow = Suji-bori double contour, check = Ichimatsu checkerboard.
  The reference mid fish is 13x9 texels, 39x27 px at 1080p. In the ink set the variant is the pattern; in the
  pigment set each variant has its own colour (stripe vermilion, spot ochre, solid green, hollow blue, check violet).
- Kujira / Shachi (the orca): 40x23 texels, 120x69 px, about three fish long. Hand-drawn and framed by a paper
  outline with a rim light, so the ink body reads on the ink water. Its facing rule has no negation.
- Chochin-Ankou (the angler): 57x51 px; its lantern lure flickers slowly between three levels.
- Kurage (jelly): bell 36-42 px wide, tentacles that wave with phase lag; worm 12x27 px; bubbles 6 and 12 px.
- Fish invert when stressed (ink body, paper pattern and outline). Fish face the way they swim (the orca's flag
  is not negated).

## Motion and rates

- The simulation steps 25 times per second; each sprite glides between steps, so motion is smooth.
- Fish animate about 2-3 frames per second; orca and angler slower; jelly bell pulses about 1.4 frames per second.
- The backdrop timer runs every 500 ms and only advances the kelp frames: each plant changes frame every 2 s
  (0.5 Hz). The water, surface and seabed are static; there are no drifting specks.
- Stress only inverts a growing share of fish from stress 0.25, trembles a share by one texel from 0.5, and above
  1.2 about a third of the fish alternate between normal and inverted at 0.8 Hz. Nothing flashes across the
  screen, and everything stops on pause.

## Measured (1920x1080, default settings, `node tests/ukiyoe-measure.js`)

- Calm backdrop: at most 0.52 % of pixels differ between frames sampled 0.1-10 s apart (limit 2 %); the fastest
  pixel changes 0.49 times per second (limit 2 Hz).
- Glide: residual of on-screen x against a straight line 0.71 px (limit 1); sprites land on whole screen pixels.
- Mean luminance 12-15 % in day, night, crowded, stress, feeding (limit 25 %). Busy 24x24 windows (period <= 2
  texels): none flagged; the worst window is 43 % busy (flag at 50 %).
- Sizes: fish 39x27 px, orca 3.08 fish lengths, angler 1.46, jelly 1.08, worm 0.31; seabed 8.9 %, tallest kelp 17.8 %.
- Stress 1.5: at most 1.63 inverted/normal flips per second per fish (limit 2); tremble at most 2.13 changes per
  second (limit 4).
