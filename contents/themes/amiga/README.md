# Amiga theme

A late-80s / early-90s Commodore Amiga (OCS/ECS) look in the spirit of James Pond, Shadow of the
Beast and Deluxe Paint art. Everything is generated by `tools/gen-amiga-theme.js` (+ `tools/amiga-backdrop.js`);
`node tools/gen-amiga-theme.js` rewrites `img/`, `--check` verifies it (run by `npm test`),
`--sheet out.png` writes an 8x contact sheet. `preview.png` is the day scene at 1/4 scale (480x270,
exactly the virtual resolution at 1080p).

## Chipset constraints that are emulated

- **12-bit colour**: every channel of every texel is a multiple of 0x11 (asserted by the generator and
  by `tests/amiga-theme.test.js`). Gradients are 12-bit levels dithered with each other, never smooth RGB.
- **Virtual grid**: art texels are shown at `vp` screen px, an integer from the screen height
  (`vpFor`: 1080p -> 4 = 480x270, 1440p -> 6, 2160p -> 8). Nothing is ever scaled by a fraction; creature
  sizes pick a hand-drawn size class instead (`kinds[].classes`). Sprite positions are snapped to the grid
  (`SNAP` in `sync.js`; slow fish therefore advance in whole-pixel steps, like Amiga sprites).
- **Copper lists**: water is one colour per scanline (800x320 texture, 1 texel = 1 virtual pixel), with an
  ordered dither only in the seam between two adjacent levels. Four lists: night, dusk/dawn, day, stress.
  They are cross-faded by `theme.js copperWeights(daylight)`.
- **Palettes**: sprites use small per-creature ramps (outline, soft outline, 3-4 body tones, belly, fin,
  marking, eye); the playfield (rocks, kelp, sand, plants) stays within ~32 colours. No alpha: translucency
  (jelly, light shafts, glow) is checkerboard / Bayer stipple.
- **Colour cycling**: the light shafts and surface ripples are 6 pre-rendered frames of a DPaint-style
  cycle range (brightness bands rotate 4 lines per frame), shown at 2.5 fps with one Image whose source switches.

## Layers (back to front)

copper lists - shafts/ripples (cycled) - far rocks (parallax drift 0.4 vp/s) - kelp + corals (4 sway frames,
two depths, 0.8 vp/s) - sand - **creatures** - near plants (4 sway frames, 1.6 vp/s) - night fade.
One 5 Hz tick (stopped when `paused`) steps the frames and the drift in whole virtual pixels.

## Creatures

Fish: 4 size classes (6x5 newborn, 9x7, 12x9, 15x10) x 5 colours x 4 tail frames; stress adds 2 flush
palettes per colour (1/3, 2/3 towards red, mixed in OKLCH and snapped to 12-bit), `red` and `alarm`.
Orca 26x13 (faces left like the simulation's flag), angler 15x12 + lure bulb/halo overlays,
jelly 2 classes (11x19, 15x25, dithered), worm 7x13, bubbles 3 classes, newborn sparkle. All 4 frames
where animated (beat from speed, bell pulse and wiggle from the simulation's phases).

## Stress and night

- Level 0..3: fish swap to pre-coloured palettes; the stress copper list fades in (quarter steps) and a red
  tint is laid over the playfield. Shake is in whole virtual pixels.
- Above 1.2 the affected fish flip between `red` and `alarm` (~1.25 Hz, desynchronised per fish); the
  background does not flash.
- Night: night copper list, a playfield fade and a light fade over everything (one blended rectangle each).

## Cost

Shared textures, pooled delegates (`EntityLayer.qml`), no per-entity layer/effect/Canvas. Fixed ambient:
4 full-screen copper images (only visible ones are drawn), 1 shaft image, ~4 tiles per strip, 3 rectangles.
